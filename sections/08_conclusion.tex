\section{Conclusion}

UAV-TAlign-12K connects captured UAV infrared-visible imagery with evaluation of pairwise evidence,
scene consensus, and operating coverage. Across its 6,037-pair official split, seven baselines
show that infrared-specific and modern matchers provide abundant homography evidence.
UAV-TAlign consolidates this evidence through robust consensus and cross-modal verification.
It produces transforms for all 15 scenes and retains nine, covering 74.4\% of evaluated
pairs. Ablations on the fixed eight-scene UAV-TAlign-1K-Lite subset identify robust consensus as the main source of improved image
agreement and reduced dispersion; resampling and controlled perturbations characterize stability
and diagnostic sensitivity. Together, the benchmark and reference framework support joint evaluation
of homography availability, transform stability, and quality-controlled scene coverage.

\section*{Data Availability}

UAV-TAlign-12K is jointly owned by Nanning Normal University and Guangxi University and is
prepared for release under the Creative Commons Attribution 4.0 International license.
The dataset contains 6,037 RGB-infrared pairs (12,074 images) across 15 scenes.
The image archive awaits repository deposit and assignment of an access
link. A separate reproducibility package contains the official manifest, metadata,
evaluation code, and numerical figure sources for submission as supplementary
material. Original UAV-TAlign software is licensed under Apache-2.0; third-party components
retain their respective licenses.
