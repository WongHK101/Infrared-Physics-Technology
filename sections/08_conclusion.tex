\section{Conclusion}

UAV-TAlign-12K connects captured UAV infrared-visible imagery with evaluation of pairwise evidence,
scene consensus, and operating coverage. Across its 6,037-pair official split, seven baselines
show that infrared-specific and modern matchers provide abundant homography evidence. The
remaining task is to consolidate that evidence and decide which scene transforms to retain.
UAV-TAlign produces transforms for all 15 scenes and retains nine, covering 74.4\% of evaluated
pairs. Cumulative ablations identify robust consensus as the main source of improved image
agreement and reduced dispersion; resampling and controlled perturbations characterize stability
and diagnostic sensitivity. Together, the dataset and reference framework make scene-scale
evidence consolidation an explicit, reproducible evaluation target for UAV infrared-visible alignment.

\section*{Data Availability}

UAV-TAlign-12K is jointly owned by Nanning Normal University and Guangxi University and is
prepared for release under the Creative Commons Attribution 4.0 International license.
The official evaluation split contains 6,037 integrity-checked RGB-infrared pairs from the
6,039-pair collection. The image archive awaits repository deposit and assignment of an access
link. A separate reproducibility package contains the official manifest, metadata, integrity
summaries, evaluation code, and numerical figure sources for submission as supplementary
material. Original UAV-TAlign software is licensed under Apache-2.0; third-party components
retain their respective licenses.
